How To Play

The Draft

A random NFL franchise is generated. Choose one eligible player from that franchise to fill one of your roster spots.

Once a franchise is used, it cannot appear again. Once a player is drafted, that player cannot be drafted again.

Example

You receive the Cowboys and choose Roger Staubach. The Cowboys are then removed from the draft pool.

Historical Players

Players can have multiple eligible positions. Historical players are evaluated using their version with the franchise you drafted.
